## Free speech

Even though Belgium rates high on [World Press Freedom Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Press_Freedom_Index), there are "racism and negationism laws" that restrict criticism of immigrants. [^1][^2][^3]

## Driving

Vehicles of total weight <3500kg do not need to buy any vignettes. However, [Liefkenshoek Tunnel is a toll road](https://www.liefkenshoektunnel.be/en/rates-subscription).
