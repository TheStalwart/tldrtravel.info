## Driving

All vehicles with at least four wheels must [buy a vignette](https://eznamka.sk/en/) to access [major motorways](https://eznamka.sk/en/specified-road-sections/network).

Street parking in Košice is can be paid for [with a number of apps](https://parking.kosice.sk/zaplatit-parkovne/).

## Motorcycling

Motorcycles and trikes are exempt from vignette requirement. No need to register or purchase any vignettes.

Car driving license (B category) only allows riding <=125cc bikes that have automatic transmission, and B-license must be at least 2 years old.[^1] Slovakian B-license also forbids riding a motorcycle abroad. For 125cc manual gearbox motorcycles and trips abroad you will need explicit A1 license.

[^1]: [Novela zákona o cestnej premávke "vodičák B na skútre do 125 ccm" prináša okrem pozitív aj riziká: motoride.sk](https://motoride.sk/c/5306/novela-zakona-o-cestnej-premavke-vodicak-b-na-skutre-do-125-ccm-prinasa-okrem-pozitiv-aj-rizika.html)
